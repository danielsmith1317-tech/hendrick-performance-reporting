        Important disclosures

        C2C uses all vehicle types. Green is 95%–115%, yellow is 85%–94.9%, and red is below 85% or above 115%. Vehicle filters still apply to other metrics, and Other / Unclassified activity stays in the totals.

        Days to Sale uses SoldDate in the selected period and original LeadDate across supplied historical exports from January 2024. It includes earlier-opened leads, deduplicates opportunities, and excludes negative or missing date pairs. These exports are snapshots; this population may differ from current CRM sold totals. Lead conversion, inactive timing, and aging retain their opening-cohort basis.
